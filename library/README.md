# Fretline free song library

`index.json` lists every song the app's **Free song library** screen offers.

- **Classical guitar** (`mutopia/*.mid`): pieces from the [Mutopia Project](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/),
  converted from the project's LilyPond sources to MIDI. Each entry keeps the typesetter credit, licence
  (Public Domain, CC BY-SA 3.0/4.0) and a link to the original piece. Works under CC BY-SA remain under that licence.
- **Folk & traditional / practice progressions**: chord charts (chords only) of public-domain songs and common
  progressions, written for Fretline.

- **Genre sets (v1.110)**: public-domain songs (traditional, or composed before 1929) as chord charts written for Fretline, in the styles Blues, Country & bluegrass, Gospel & spirituals, Jazz & ragtime, Celtic & sea shanties, Latin & flamenco, plus "Rock & metal (original riffs)": short tabs (`kind:'tab'`, `src` is tab text, `*` after a fret = palm mute) that are original to Fretline. Levels 1-3 = easy to hard. Only add songs that are traditional or published before 1929 (US public domain); never copy chord charts or tabs from commercial songs.
