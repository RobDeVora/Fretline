🧪 Split a song

Test of Demucs (Meta's open-source source-separation model, 6-stem version) running on this device: it pulls the guitar out of a recording, and leaves you the band without the guitar to practise over. Your audio never leaves the phone. The model is downloaded once (a few hundred MB, so use Wi-Fi) and kept for next time.

The StemSplit builds use a number type (64-bit "double") the web runtime can't run, so they stop with "Could not find an implementation". The SonicDemixer build is the same model exported differently.
The song is saved to Fretline's My songs with the band (without the guitar) as its backing track.
Add the lyrics (optional)
Paste the lyrics, one line per sung line (or load a text file). The Lab listens to where the singing is in the recording and times each line and word to it, so they show on the highway with 🎤 Sing. It works best on clear lead vocals. Chords in [brackets] and headings like [Chorus] are ignored.
Use a tab or chord sheet for the notes (optional)
Paste the song's tab or chord sheet (or load a text file). Its notes or chords are used as written, and lined up with the recording's guitar, instead of the AI writing the notes down.
Text, ChordPro, PDF, MIDI, MusicXML or Guitar Pro.
The sheet should match this recording (same intro, verses and repeats). Parts the recording doesn't play are skipped; parts the sheet doesn't cover get no notes.
And the other one, for a song with a solo (optional)
Put the tab (the solo) above and the chord sheet here, or the other way round. Both are lined up with the recording: you play the chords as strums and the tab's notes where it has them.
A file here is used as whatever the first one isn't: chords if the first is a tab, notes if it's a chord sheet. Files (MIDI, Guitar Pro, MusicXML) line up by their own timing, which is the most exact.
Where is the tab in the song? Leave these empty to let the Lab find it, or type when it plays (like 1:05 and 1:30).
You can leave the app while it works: a notification shows progress, and another says when your song is ready. Keep the app open in the background (do not swipe it away).
